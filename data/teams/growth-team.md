# Growth Team

**Type:** Team
**Aliases:** Growth, the growth group

## Overview

The Growth Team owns the merchant-facing surfaces and the systems that keep
merchants informed: the dashboard they log into and the notifications they
receive.

## Ownership

The Merchant Dashboard is owned by the Growth Team.
The Notification Service is owned by the Growth Team.

## Responsibilities

The Growth Team owns front-end performance for the Merchant Dashboard and the
deliverability of transactional email, SMS, and webhooks sent by the
Notification Service.

## Dependencies on other teams

The Growth Team's systems consume the Public REST API and the User API, and the
Notification Service subscribes to transaction events published by the Ledger
Service.
